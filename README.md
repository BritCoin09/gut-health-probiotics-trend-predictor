# 🦠 Gut Health & 🌿 Probiotics Trend Predictor

Forecasting microbiome interest using Google Trends + Prophet
This project analyzes five years of Google Trends data for gut health and probiotics, then uses Facebook Prophet to forecast future interest. All charts follow a Seed Health–aligned visual design, creating a clean, wellness‑focused aesthetic.

### 🌍 Why This Matters
Search interest reflects real consumer curiosity. Understanding how gut‑health and probiotic attention rises and falls helps wellness brands:

- Time product launches

- Plan educational content

- Anticipate seasonal demand

- Respond quickly to emerging microbiome trends

Google Trends acts as an early‑signal tool for strategic decision‑making.

### 📊 Project Overview

This notebook performs:

- Google Trends data extraction using Pytrends

- Weekly time‑series cleaning and smoothing

- Visual trend analysis with Seed Health palette

- Correlation analysis between gut health & probiotics

- Prophet forecasting for 6‑month projections

- Business‑ready insights for wellness strategy

### 📏 Y‑Axis Scale Guide

Google Trends uses a normalized 0–100 scale:

- 0 — no measurable interest

- 20 — very low

- 40 — moderate

- 60 — strong

- 80 — very strong

- 100 — peak interest

This scale applies to all charts in the project.

### 🔍 Key Findings

**1. Gut‑Health Interest Over Time**

- Clear seasonal spikes (New Year’s wellness resets)

- Strong growth over the 5‑year period

- Major surge in early 2026 due to microbiome breakthroughs

**2. Correlation Between Gut Health & Probiotics**

Gut health and probiotics show a correlation of 0.91, meaning interest in both topics rises together.

**3. Forecast Results**

Prophet forecasts indicate:

- Continued growth through 2026–2027

- Parallel rising interest in both gut health and probiotics

- Strong alignment between broad wellness curiosity and specific probiotic solutions

### ⚠️ Limitations

- Google Trends provides normalized interest, not absolute search volume

- Weekly data limits seasonality decomposition

- Viral events can disrupt forecast patterns

- Search interest reflects curiosity, not purchasing behavior
